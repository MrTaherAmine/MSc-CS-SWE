04 / OPEN METHODOLOGY

Understand the model.

Transparent, intentionally bounded scoring that supports decisions rather than pretending to measure the full cyber risk landscape.

M1 · DATA

Recognized reference points

NIST CSF 2.0 and its Small Business Quick-Start Guide shape the six-function model. CISA CPGs inform pragmatic improvement actions.

M2 · SCORING

Simple by design

Each function has three items rated from 0 to 4. Function score = total / 12 × 100. The overall score is the mean of six rounded function scores.

M3 · INTERPRETATION

No misleading assurance

Initial: 0–39; Developing: 40–59; Defined: 60–79; Adaptive: 80–100. These project-specific labels are not official NIST tiers.
